Safety Factors

Forseti evaluates safety using a holistic framework aligned with Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs. Each dimension represents essential elements that contribute to community well-being and personal security.

Comprehensive Assessment

Each dimension is measured using multiple data sources and indicators to provide a complete picture of community safety and wellness.

The Seven Dimensions of Safety

Click on each dimension to explore the factors we monitor:

Security & Protection

The foundation of Maslow's hierarchy - physical safety and freedom from harm. This dimension tracks crime, emergency response, and environmental hazards.

Safety Factors:
  • Violent Crime Rate
  • Property Crime Rate
  • Emergency Response Time
  • Police Presence & Community Relations
  • Street Lighting & Infrastructure
  • Environmental Hazards
  • Traffic Safety
  • Building Code Compliance
  • Fire Safety & Prevention
  • Disaster Preparedness

Vitality & Basic Needs

Building on physical safety - access to essential resources like housing, food, and financial stability that energize daily life.

Forseti can learn what matters most to you about your neighborhood's vitality. Talk with Forseti →

Safety Factors:
  • Housing Quality & Affordability
  • Food Security & Access
  • Employment Opportunities
  • Income Stability
  • Utility Reliability
  • Financial Well-being Indicators

Community & Belonging

Social connections and community cohesion - the networks and relationships that create belonging and mutual support.

Forseti can help you discover community connections. Talk with Forseti →

Safety Factors:
  • Neighborhood Social Cohesion
  • Community Organization Presence
  • Social Support Networks
  • Civic Participation Rate
  • Cultural & Religious Institutions
  • Community Events & Gathering Spaces
  • Social Capital Indicators

Autonomy & Rights

Freedom of movement, privacy, and justice - the ability to make choices and exercise rights without undue constraint.

Forseti can explore what freedom means in your community context. Talk with Forseti →

Safety Factors:
  • Freedom of Movement
  • Privacy Protection
  • Access to Justice
  • Police Accountability
  • Fair Housing Practices
  • Anti-discrimination Measures

Mastery & Development

Education, skills, and economic opportunity - the resources and systems that enable personal and collective growth.

Forseti can identify opportunities for skill development in your area. Talk with Forseti →

Safety Factors:
  • Education Quality & Access
  • Skills Training & Development
  • Economic Mobility
  • Job Market Strength
  • Entrepreneurship Support
  • Safety Training & Preparedness

Purpose & Contribution

Civic engagement and contribution - the ability to make meaningful contributions to community well-being and feel valued.

Forseti can suggest ways to contribute to community safety. Talk with Forseti →

Safety Factors:
  • Volunteer Opportunities
  • Civic Engagement Programs
  • Community Leadership
  • Neighborhood Improvement Initiatives
  • Public Service Accessibility
  • Community Voice in Decision-Making

Holistic Health & Identity

Comprehensive well-being - mental health, physical health, and community identity that create a sense of wholeness and fulfillment.

Forseti can discuss holistic community health with you. Talk with Forseti →

Safety Factors:
  • Mental Health Resources
  • Physical Health Services
  • Substance Abuse Support
  • Recreation & Green Space
  • Air & Water Quality
  • Community Identity & Pride
  • Cultural Vitality
  • Demographic Stability

How Forseti Uses These Dimensions

  • We analyze these seven dimensions across every neighborhood in Philadelphia using data from multiple sources.
  • Our AI combines real-time feeds with historical patterns to assess current safety conditions.
  • When you enter an area with concerning indicators across multiple dimensions, we send contextual safety alerts.
  • Understanding the full picture helps you make informed decisions about your safety.
